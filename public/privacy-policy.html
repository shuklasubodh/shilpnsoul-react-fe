Privacy Policy

Effective 16 September 2026

Shilp & Soul operates an online store for curated craft and home products. This policy explains how we handle personal information when you browse our site, create an account, place an order, contact us, or choose to receive messages.

Information we collect

We collect the details you provide, such as your name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, delivery address, account credentials, order details, product requests, and messages to our support team. We also receive technical information needed to operate and secure the site, such as browser and device information and server logs. Payment providers process payment information; we receive payment status and transaction references needed to fulfill orders.

How we use information

We use this information to create and manage accounts, process payments and orders, arrange delivery, send order and account notifications by your selected channel (email, SMS, or WhatsApp), respond to requests, prevent misuse, and meet recordkeeping obligations. We send marketing messages only when you register for them. You can ask us to stop marketing messages through our data request form.

Sharing

We share information with service providers only as needed to run the store, including payment, hosting, delivery, and messaging providers. If you choose WhatsApp as a notification channel, the information needed to send that message is processed through Meta's WhatsApp service. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect our services and customers.

Storage and retention

We keep personal information while needed for your account, orders, support requests, security, and applicable legal or accounting requirements. We remove or anonymize information when it is no longer needed, subject to required retention. We use reasonable security measures to protect stored information.

Your choices and requests

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information, or ask about how it is used, through the data deletion and privacy request form. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. Some order and transaction records may need to be retained where required by law.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as our services change. The date above shows when this version took effect. For privacy questions, use our request form.

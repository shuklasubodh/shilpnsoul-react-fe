Request deletion of your data

Effective 16 September 2026

You can request deletion of your Shilp & Soul account and associated personal data, including information used for WhatsApp notifications. You do not need to sign in to make a request.

  1. Complete the form below using the email address associated with your account or order.
  2. State whether you want your account and personal data deleted, or only want a specific type of data removed. Include an order number or WhatsApp number if it helps us locate the record.
  3. Our support team will contact you by email to verify your identity if needed and explain the outcome.

We will delete or anonymize information that is no longer needed. Some order, payment, or transaction records may have to be retained for legal, accounting, fraud prevention, or dispute purposes. We will explain any such retention in our reply.
